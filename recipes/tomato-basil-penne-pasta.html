Tomato Basil Penne Pasta

Description

Tomato Basil Penne Pasta is a delicious Italian dish that combines al dente penne pasta with a flavorful tomato sauce infused with fresh basil. The sauce is made from ripe tomatoes, garlic, olive oil, and herbs, creating a rich and aromatic flavor that perfectly complements the pasta.

This dish is known for its bright, tangy taste and is often enjoyed as a main course. It can be served with grated Parmesan cheese on top and pairs well with a side salad or garlic bread.

Ingredients

  • 12 ounces penne pasta
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil leaves, chopped
  • Grated Parmesan cheese for serving (optional)

Steps

  1. Cook the penne pasta according to the package instructions until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a large skillet, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the crushed tomatoes to the skillet, along with the dried oregano, salt, and pepper. Stir to combine.
  4. Simmer the tomato sauce for 15-20 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld together.
  5. Stir in the chopped fresh basil leaves into the sauce just before serving.
  6. Toss the cooked penne pasta with the tomato basil sauce until well coated.
  7. Serve immediately, garnished with grated Parmesan cheese if desired.
