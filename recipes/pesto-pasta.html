Pesto Pasta

Description

Pesto pasta is a flavorful Italian dish made with a sauce called pesto, which is traditionally made from fresh basil leaves, garlic, pine nuts, Parmesan cheese, and olive oil. The sauce is blended together to create a vibrant green, aromatic mixture that is tossed with cooked pasta.

This dish is known for its bright, herbaceous flavor and creamy texture. Pesto pasta can be served as a main course or as a side dish, and it pairs well with grilled chicken, shrimp, or roasted vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups fresh basil leaves
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1/4 cup pine nuts
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 12 ounces pasta (such as spaghetti, fettuccine, or penne)

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a food processor, combine the basil leaves, garlic, pine nuts, and Parmesan cheese. Pulse until finely chopped.
  3. With the food processor running, slowly drizzle in the olive oil until the pesto sauce is smooth and well combined. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  4. In a large bowl, toss the cooked pasta with the pesto sauce until the pasta is evenly coated.
  5. Serve immediately, garnished with additional Parmesan cheese and fresh basil leaves if desired.
