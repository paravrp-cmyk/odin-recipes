Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a hearty Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich tomato sauce, seasoned meat, and melted cheese. Baked until golden and bubbly, it is known for its warm, comforting flavor and satisfying texture.

This classic meal is popular at family dinners and special gatherings. With its cheesy layers and savory filling, lasagna is both delicious and comforting, often served with salad or garlic bread.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cans (6 ounces each) tomato paste
  • 2 teaspoons dried basil leaves
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 12 lasagna noodles, cooked and drained
  • 16 ounces ricotta cheese
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 3/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef and drain excess fat.
  4. Add the onion and garlic, cooking until softened.
  5. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, basil, salt, and pepper.
  6. Simmer the sauce for 15 minutes.
  7. In a mixing bowl, combine the ricotta cheese and half of the mozzarella cheese.
  8. Spread a thin layer of sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish.
  9. Layer half of the noodles, then half of the cheese mixture, and then half of the sauce.
  10. Repeat the layers once more.
  11. Cover with the remaining mozzarella cheese.
  12. Bake for 30 minutes or until bubbly and golden brown.
